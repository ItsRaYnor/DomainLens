Who registered a domain, through which registrar, and since when — without running a scan. A subdomain is looked up at its registered domain.

Most registries withhold the holder and contact details under the GDPR. Where they do, this page says so and links to the registry's own lookup, which may show more.

Watchlist

Domains you want once they come free. DomainLens looks each one up at the registry — every six hours, hourly while it is being deleted, and every minute in the hour a .nl domain leaves quarantine — and sends every change through your notification channels (Settings → Notifications). It cannot register a domain for you: when one is available, register it at your registrar straight away, or have a backorder service catch it.

Paced so no registry blocks this server: at most one request every two seconds per registry, and a registry that asks us to slow down is left alone for as long as it asks.
