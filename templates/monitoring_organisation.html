Your companies and the units under them, each with both kinds of monitoring: security (scans, monitors and what they found) and registration (quarantine, expiry, registrar). A domain belongs to one unit; every scan and monitor of its hostnames — www., shop., mail. — counts there too. The figures of a unit include the units below it.

Put a domain in a unit from the domain portfolio (paste or import, with a path such as Org X > Sales), when adding a monitor, or from a scan result.

Contacts

The people to ask about a company, a business unit and its domains. Link a contact to a unit; the units and domains below it have that contact unless they name their own. A contact is kept once, so someone who leaves is replaced in one place.

Edit, move or merge units

Notify: e-mail addresses that get the alerts about this unit's domains — registration and security — besides the general channels. A unit's alerts also go to the addresses of the units above it, so a company contact hears about its business units. Sent through the e-mail settings under Settings → Notifications, with the same minimum severity.

E-mail notifications are not set up (Settings → Notifications), so unit addresses are kept but nothing is sent to them yet.

A unit may name the registrar its domains belong at; units below it inherit that unless they name their own. It is matched against the registrar and the reseller, without regard to case; several names may be given, separated by commas. Deleting a unit moves its domains and units one level up.

Merge two units

For a reorganisation: every domain and unit of the first goes to the second, and the first is removed. Where both have a unit of the same name, those are merged too. The second keeps its expected registrar, and takes the first's only when it had none.
