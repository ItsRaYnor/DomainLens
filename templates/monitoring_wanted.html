Domains you want, not ones you hold (those belong in the domain portfolio): taken, expiring or in quarantine, watched until they come free. DomainLens looks each one up at the registry — every six hours, hourly while it is being deleted, and every minute in the hour a .nl domain leaves quarantine — and sends every change through your notification channels (Settings → Notifications). It cannot register a domain for you: when one is available, register it at your registrar straight away, or have a backorder service catch it.
