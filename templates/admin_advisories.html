Export and import

Export puts your own rules in one file, to move them to another DomainLens or keep them under version control. Import adds the rules of such a file: all of them or, when one cannot be used, none.

Known CVEs (NVD, NCSC-NL)

Beside the rules, every scan can compare the version a site shows with all known CVEs of PHP, nginx, the Apache HTTP Server, lighttpd, OpenSSL, WordPress and Drupal, from the National Vulnerability Database. One finding per product, not one per CVE. Off by default: switch it on under Admin → Settings → CVE data. Synced once a day; data older than the setting allows is reported as not measured, never as no known CVEs.
