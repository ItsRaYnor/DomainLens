The domains you hold, per organisation unit (company, business unit, department): at which registrar each one is, until when it is registered, and whether it is in quarantine. Give a unit the registrar its domains belong at, and every domain that is elsewhere is marked to move. DomainLens looks each domain up at its registry — daily, every six hours in the month before it expires, hourly while it is being deleted — and sends every change through your notification channels (Settings → Notifications).

Each domain also has a decision — keep and renew (the default), to decide, cancel, or request or claim — and a contact. The warnings follow the decision: an expiry is news for a domain you keep, and the plan for one you cancel. Whether a domain is in use (it receives or sends mail, or has a web address) is measured with each lookup; a domain in use belongs with your threat intelligence service, one that is not in use does not need it.

Add domains

Paste one domain per line, optionally followed by its unit (example.nl;Sales, or a path: example.nl;Org X > Sales; missing levels are created), or choose a CSV, text or Excel (.xlsx) file. With a header row that names the columns — for example Domain and Company, or Domein and Bedrijf (or Unit, Eenheid) — only those columns are used and the others are ignored. A subdomain or web address is added as its registered domain (https://shop.example.nl/x becomes example.nl), and the result lists what was converted. A domain already in the portfolio moves to the unit named for it; lines without a unit go to the unit chosen below. At most 2,000 domains in all.

A header row may also name a Decision (Besluit) column — keep, to decide, cancel, or request/claim, in English or Dutch — a Contact (Contactpersoon) column with a name, an e-mail address or "Name <address>", an E-mail column, and a Threat intel column (yes or no). A contact is found by e-mail address or name, made from the account with that address, or added. An empty cell changes nothing.

Units, their place in the organisation, the registrar each should be at and their contacts are managed under Organisation. A domain has the contact of its unit unless you link one to the domain itself.
