The domains you hold, grouped per company or department: at which registrar each one is, until when it is registered, and whether it is in quarantine. Give a group the registrar its domains belong at, and every domain that is elsewhere is marked to move. DomainLens looks each domain up at its registry — daily, every six hours in the month before it expires, hourly while it is being deleted — and sends every change through your notification channels (Settings → Notifications).

The scheduler is switched off, so nothing is looked up automatically. Switch it on under Monitoring, or use “Check now”.

Add domains

Paste one domain per line, optionally followed by its group (example.nl;Sales), or choose a CSV, text or Excel (.xlsx) file. With a header row that names the columns — for example Domain and Company, or Domein and Bedrijf — only those columns are used and the others are ignored. A subdomain or web address is added as its registered domain (https://shop.example.nl/x becomes example.nl), and the result lists what was converted. A domain already in the portfolio moves to the group named for it; lines without a group go to the group chosen below. At most 2,000 domains in all.

Groups and their registrar

The expected registrar is matched against both the registrar and the reseller the registry publishes, without regard to case; several names may be given, separated by commas. Leave it empty and the group's domains are not judged on it.
