## Monitoring: no F while DNS is down, and scans at a time you choose

### Scans wait while DNS does not answer

A scan that could not reach DNS still ran every check, found nothing, and rated the domain F
for an outage rather than for anything it had configured. Now, when a scan's first lookup gets
no answer (a timeout or SERVFAIL), DNS is asked again and the two causes are told apart:

- **This server's own DNS does not answer** (the container's network or its resolver): every
  due scan waits and runs as soon as DNS answers again. The monitoring page says since when.
  Nothing counts as a failed scan and no events are raised.
- **Only the domain's name servers do not answer**: the monitor tries again after 15 minutes.
  After four tries one event (*No answer from the name servers of …*, medium) says so, and the
  monitor waits for its next regular run.

Either way no scan and no rating is stored. A scan started by hand says why it was not run;
`POST /api/scan` answers `503`. A name that does not exist (NXDOMAIN) is reported as before.

### A time of day per monitor

**Schedule…** on a monitor, or the new time field when adding one, sets the time it scans at,
in the time zone of the browser that set it. A daily scan stays at its hour after a late or
manual run, also across the change to and from summer time. Shorter intervals count from that
time. The monitor list shows the schedule and the next scan.

### Plan all scans one after another

Under **Scheduler**, **Plan all scans** gives every monitor its own time from a start time, a set
number of minutes apart (by default 2): 10:00, 10:02, 10:04 … so they do not all run at once.
Monitors added later, from any source, take the first free time.

**Re-plan all scans** puts every monitor of every unit back in line in one batch: it closes the
gaps left by removed monitors and takes in monitors that were added out of order or given a time
of their own. The same batch can set one frequency for all monitors. The status under the
button says when re-planning is needed, and **Remove the times** drops the plan.

### API

- `PATCH /api/monitors/{id}` and `POST /api/monitors` take `scan_time` (`"HH:MM"`, or `""` to
  drop it) and `scan_tz` (an IANA time zone).
- `POST /api/monitors/plan` with `start`, `spacing_minutes`, `scan_tz` and optionally
  `schedule_minutes`; `DELETE /api/monitors/plan`. `GET /api/monitors` reports the plan under
  `plan`.

### Upgrading

Nothing to do. The database gains two columns on start-up, and the image adds the `tzdata`
package. Monitors without a time keep running one interval after their last run, as before.
