## A dangling CNAME is reported as one

A name that is a CNAME to a target that no longer exists was reported as *Domain is not in DNS
(NXDOMAIN)*, with advice to check the delegation at the registrar. The resolver follows the CNAME
and answers NXDOMAIN for the whole chain; that answer belongs to the target, not to the scanned
name. The dangling record itself -- where a subdomain takeover starts -- went unmentioned.

The scan now tells the two apart. For a dangling CNAME it reports what the takeover audit reports
for the same record: *Possible subdomain takeover* when the target is at a provider where the name
can be claimed again, otherwise *Dangling CNAME*, with the target, the evidence and the fix:
remove or repoint the record. Mail, web and TLS checks are not run, since the name resolves to
nothing. The scan page says which name the CNAME points to. Once the target resolves again, the
name is scanned as usual.
